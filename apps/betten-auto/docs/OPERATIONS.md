# Betten Imports Inventory Desk

This private application is an independent inventory workflow implementation. It is not affiliated with vAuto and does not reproduce its licensed market dataset, proprietary scores, or valuation models.

Inventory, local comparable samples, internal price plans, cost worksheets, appraisals, recon tasks, description drafts, sourcing opportunities, wholesale records, audit history, and backups are implemented. The Site uses server-side D1 storage. Browser preferences are not the system of record. Optimistic revisions reject conflicting edits.

Local pricing matches observed asking-price listings by normalized make/model, condition, model-year range, mileage range, freshness, and optional exact trim and known distance. Mercedes model aliases retain numeric engine/powertrain variants. Price rank covers the selected sample including the Betten vehicle. At least three matching comparables are required for rank and percentage. It does not establish sold values or full-market coverage. Different dealer fees and incentives can affect comparisons; the displayed price basis must be reviewed. Unknown distance is excluded when a radius is selected.

Published listings are sourced from the combined Betten Imports website. Original inventory dates are not verified acquisition dates; user-entered acquisition dates drive stock age. Costs, actual recon, confirmed sales, and price approval are entered by the dealership. Blank expense lines contribute zero only to the labeled estimate. Public disappearance archives an observed listing without declaring it sold. All internal records remain stored.

Approved price plans export as CSV. They do not write to Dealer.com or VinSolutions. The dealer must configure an authorized feed/API with those providers before automatic publishing can be added. Vehicle history and auction links require the appropriate provider access. No auction bids or purchases are placed.

## Appraisal market pricing

Appraisals include structured vehicle identity, VIN decoding and an optional inventory selector. Average asking price is the arithmetic mean of current matched listings; median, range, sample size, dealer/source count and source links remain visible. The filters use the same make/model variant, condition, model-year range, mileage range, observation freshness and optional exact trim. VIN and canonical listing URL both deduplicate the sample. The current source sample has no verified geographic radius and does not cover the full market.

A suggested list-price starting point is the sample median rounded to $100, available only with at least three matching listings. Apply either that figure or the average explicitly to expected retail; filtering never silently changes the user's retail plan. The retail-back worksheet subtracts the entered target gross and expenses to calculate the maximum offer. Inspect condition, equipment, fees and incentives before making a final dealership decision; no automated valuation adjustments are made.

Saving an appraisal preserves the price summary, comparison time, filters, source count and up to 200 valid comparison VINs alongside the worksheet. Editing recomputes current market prices and separately displays the saved basis. Printing includes the current price sample and criteria. Internal acquisition costs and notes remain private in D1.

## Dealer cost/date CSV

Data & connections accepts VIN-matched cost and acquisition-date CSV exports. The default fills only missing fields and preserves existing values, including a verified zero. Unknown or duplicate VINs and invalid amounts/dates reject the entire file. Optimistic revisions abort the whole transaction when another session edits a target record during import. No source listing price or disposition is replaced by this endpoint. Download the column template in the app; provider exports may need column mapping.

The public listing seeds are served as static JSON assets and loaded through the Worker's internal ASSETS binding. Run node ops/prepare-seeds.mjs before building after a seed change. This avoids bundling large vehicle datasets into executable code. Only public listing material belongs in those assets; costs, notes and other private dealership records stay in D1.

The wider-area comparison workflow is documented in EXPANDED_MARKET.md. Pricing and new appraisals default to same-year/equivalent-trim matches and allow explicit broader criteria. Preserve lib/seed-expanded-comps.json through regional refreshes, recheck those official listing URLs, and retain failed-source observations with their original timestamps. Current scopes are selected source datasets, not nationwide census coverage. Average and median asking-price ratios are displayed separately and have no proprietary valuation adjustments.

Optional licensed market integration is described in MARKET_DATA.md. An Auto.dev key is configured for Free evaluation only; no production subscription is active. Automated public collection uses no Auto.dev credits. Public samples remain limited coverage. Do not claim every current/new vehicle has nationwide matches. `ops/run-market-refresh.mjs` resumes licensed jobs only when account and storage rights permit; evaluation mode makes no fleet queries.

## Inventory workspace

Inventory has separate New and Used controls based on the imported condition, including certified vehicles on Used. Search, status, make, sorting, paging and inventory export apply to the selected side. Operational rows show stock/VIN, available body details, current website ask, entered cost, internal retail plan and a clearly labeled saved-sample market comparison. Missing costs and acquisition dates stay unavailable. This table is not a live national feed or a source of proprietary book values.

Selecting a vehicle opens its Pricing tab inside Inventory. The retail plan, selling reserve, approval and computed gross use the same Save vehicle action as other vehicle fields. Live and saved comparison panels remain distinct, and applying an average or list-price suggestion changes the draft plan only. Data & connections retains the saved-comparison library/import tools. Approved plans export from Inventory; no prices are sent to dealership posting providers.

The blue/gray classic workspace and horizontal desktop workflow navigation are an independent layout intended for familiar dealership use. They do not represent a Cox/vAuto affiliation or import licensed data from the supplied screenshot.

Optional licensed-provider searches require an explicit Get market comparisons action; shared public dealer comparisons refresh through the daily collector without a per-vehicle button. For the licensed provider, opening vehicles and changing criteria are free of provider calls. ## vAuto Excel cost import
Data & connections accepts the native .xlsx vAuto vehicle report (CSV remains supported). Parsing happens in the browser; the workbook is not uploaded or stored. Exactly one sheet must have a VIN and recognized financial/date header within its first 100 rows. Extra report fields, Age, Last $ Change, Price, market ratios, disposition and CARFAX flags are ignored. VIN and Cost map directly; formatted acquisition date cells are supported when provided. Blank costs remain unknown and zero is retained. Invalid values, duplicate VINs/financial columns and ambiguous worksheets fail before import. Parsed financial rows go through the existing atomic /api/dealer-import validation and fill-missing policy; existing dealer records are preserved. Limit: 20 MB, 5,000 vehicle rows. Legacy .xls and encrypted workbooks are not supported. The pasted text attachment was used to confirm column names; no real workbook was provided in this turn, and no actual dealership financial records were imported.

### Nonstandard wholesale identifiers and unmatched inventory
The financial report reader skips nonstandard VIN/serials only for explicitly Wholesale rows and lists their exact Excel row, stock, identity and reason. Retail VIN errors still stop the upload. The UI opts into skipUnknown on the dealer-import endpoint: valid VINs absent from both current and archived inventory are omitted and returned in skippedUnknownVins. The default endpoint remains strict. No inventory, disposition, cost or notes are created for omitted rows; user-entered values remain protected. The supplied October 23 workbook parses to 310 standard-VIN rows plus 3 nonstandard wholesale exceptions; a read-only hosted inventory check found 16 of those 310 not in current/archived inventory. No actual financial import was executed during this fix.

Age-based stock dates: Excel Age is now converted to acquiredDate only with a valid report date, selected by the user or parsed from YYYY-MM-DD in the filename. acquiredDate = reportDate minus Age calendar days. The server validates this evidence and records acquiredDateSource=vauto_report_age, stockAgeReportDate and stockAgeAtReport only when filling a missing date. Existing dates and their provenance are preserved. The supplied report filename gives 2026-10-23. This is a derived stock date, not a separately confirmed acquisition transaction date.
